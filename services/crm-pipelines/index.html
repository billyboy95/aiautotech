How we implement

  1. 01
    Connect sources

    WhatsApp, website forms, email, calls and ad leads flow into one CRM, with duplicates merged.

  2. 02
    Build pipeline & automations

    Stages that match how you actually sell, auto-assignment, follow-up reminders and nurturing messages.

  3. 03
    Launch & train

    We onboard your team, set up dashboards and review the numbers with you after the first month.

How you gain

  • No enquiry lost in someone's personal phone or inbox
  • Faster follow-ups with automatic reminders and replies
  • Your full pipeline visible at a glance
  • Know which marketing channel actually brings paying clients
  • Notes, tasks and messages logged automatically, so less admin
  • Owners see the numbers without chasing staff for updates

Who it's for

Built for South African businesses that live on enquiries, bookings and follow-ups.

  • Clinics & practices

    Track new-patient enquiries from first message through to the first visit.

  • Schools & colleges

    Applicants from enquiry to enrolment, with a document checklist per student.

    Buyers, sellers and tenants in separate pipelines with viewing follow-ups.

  • Brokers & advisers

    Quotes, renewals and cross-sell opportunities with reminder dates.

  • Service businesses

    Quotes sent, accepted and invoiced, so you always know what is outstanding.
