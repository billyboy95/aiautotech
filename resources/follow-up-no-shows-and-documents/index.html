No-shows, warm leads going cold, and the document chase

The enquiry already arrived. The revenue is in the second message, the third, and the missing file.

AI AutoTech (Pty) Ltd · About 3 min read · From the 2026 guide

Following up no-shows and cancellations

Someone misses the chair, the viewing or the consult. The gap is already in the diary. A short WhatsApp the same day, with a new time, is the job. A no-show follow-up agent sends that message with open times, in a friendly tone you have approved.

The guide's typical estimate is 1–3 hours a week. It fits dental practices, clinics, salons and driving schools. The message is an offer to rebook. It is not a lecture.

Qualifying and following up leads

The useful questions are specific: budget, area, timing, whether a bond is pre-approved, what cover is needed. Then someone has to follow up the quote or the viewing before the lead goes cold.

A qualifier tags each lead hot, warm or cold in the CRM pipeline. A follow-up agent checks in after the viewing or the quote, and keeps colder leads in touch with something relevant, such as a new listing that matches what they asked for. Typical estimate in the guide: 3–8 hours a week. It fits estate agents, brokers, trades and other B2B service businesses.

CRM and pipelines are where those tags live, so the follow-up is attached to a person and a next step, not a forgotten chat.

Collecting and chasing documents

“Please send your ID copy… and the licence disc… and the current schedule.” Then a reminder. Then another. A document collector asks for exactly what is needed on WhatsApp, follows up until it arrives, and hands the team a complete file.

It does not give advice. Advice and claims stay with licensed staff. Typical estimate: 2–6 hours a week. It fits insurance brokers, colleges handling applications, accountants and rental agents. The Broker Client Team and the College Admissions Team are built around this chase.

Planning ranges, not guarantees. These are typical estimates from the 2026 guide for a small South African service business. Actual time depends on your volumes, your process and the setup.

South African reality check

Most of this runs on WhatsApp because that is where your customers already are. Read the ground rules on approved wording, human handoff and POPIA before you automate a document request. An ID copy is personal information. Ask for it when the process needs it, say why, and keep the file with the people who are allowed to see it.
