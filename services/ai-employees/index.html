Handle the busy work so your team can focus on what matters.

How we implement

  1. 01
    Map the job

    We sit with you and write down exactly what the role does: which questions it answers, which tasks it completes and when it must hand over to a person.

  2. 02
    Build & connect

    We train the agent on your prices, policies and FAQs, then connect it to WhatsApp, email, your calendar and your CRM.

  3. 03
    Launch & supervise

    A soft launch with your team watching, then monthly reviews where we tune its answers and add new tasks as you grow.

How you gain

  • Enquiries answered in seconds, including evenings, weekends and public holidays
  • Consistent answers based on your real prices, policies and service areas
  • Every conversation logged, so nothing depends on someone's memory
  • Clear hand-over to a person for complaints, sales closes and anything sensitive
  • Extra capacity without recruitment, UIF, leave or weeks of training
  • A flat monthly fee in Rand that you can budget for

Who it's for

Built for South African businesses that live on enquiries, bookings and follow-ups.

  • Clinics & practices

    A front-desk agent that answers 'are you open?', 'do you take my medical aid?' and books consultation slots.

  • Schools & colleges

    An admissions agent that explains programmes, fees and entry requirements and collects applicant details.

  • Estate agents

    A lead agent that qualifies buyers and tenants on budget, area and bond pre-approval before you call.

  • Brokers & advisers

    An intake agent that gathers the details needed for a quote and politely chases missing documents.

  • Service businesses

    A booking and quoting agent for plumbers, electricians, salons, repair shops and consultants.
