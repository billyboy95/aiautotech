The 10 jobs your business should hand to AI this year

South African edition, 2026

A free guide from AI AutoTech (Pty) Ltd in Benoni, Gauteng. It is written for South African owners of dental practices, estate agencies, brokerages and other service businesses, and covers what to automate in a world of WhatsApp-first customers and Rand budgets.

Inside the guide

  1. First replies on WhatsApp and your website
  2. Answering the calls you miss
  3. Booking, moving and cancelling appointments
  4. Confirmations and reminders
  5. Following up no-shows and cancellations
  6. Qualifying and following up leads
  7. Collecting and chasing documents
  8. Renewals, recalls and repeat business
  9. Asking for Google reviews
  10. Quotes, invoices, CRM updates and reports
