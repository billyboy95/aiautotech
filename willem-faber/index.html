Willem Frederik (Billy) Faber

Founder & Managing Director, AI AutoTech

Billy Faber leads AI AutoTech, a South African company that builds AI receptionists, WhatsApp automation and CRM systems. His work focuses on helping teams answer enquiries faster, follow up with leads and book appointments.

Book a conversation See AI AutoTech's work LinkedIn profile

How he works

Billy starts with the daily tasks that take up staff time. His team then connects automation to the tools a business already uses, with a handover to staff when a customer needs personal help.

He also leads product strategy and workflow design for AI AutoTech OS, which brings AI assistants, lead capture, booking, and reporting into one platform.

Selected work

AI AutoTech's published education projects include websites for EASTC Holdings, EASTC Foundation NPC and EASTECH Institute. These sites help visitors find programmes, admissions information and scholarship opportunities.
