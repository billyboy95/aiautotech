Recalls, Google reviews, and the Friday spreadsheet

A lot of next year's work is already sitting in the client list and the spreadsheet.

AI AutoTech (Pty) Ltd · About 3 min read · From the 2026 guide

Renewals, recalls and repeat business

Six-month check-up recalls, policy renewals, lease renewals and “time for your service” messages are revenue already in the client list. A renewal reminder agent contacts the client ahead of the date and books a review call or appointment with the right person on your team.

The guide's typical estimate is 1–3 hours a week. It fits brokers, dental practices, property managers and vehicle services. The agent books the conversation. The licensed or clinical person still has it.

Asking for Google reviews

The job is simple and easy to forget: ask every happy customer for a review, with a direct link, while the visit or the deal is still fresh. A reviews agent sends a short WhatsApp after the appointment, with your Google review link.

Typical estimate: 1–2 hours a week. It fits any local business that wins work from Google Maps. Ask people who had the service. Skip invented reviews and paid reviews.

Quotes, invoices, CRM updates and reports

Retyping the same details into a spreadsheet, building the weekly report, sending a standard quote or invoice, and onboarding a new client are the Friday jobs. Business automation plus a CRM pipeline puts every lead in one place, sends quotes and invoices from templates you approve, and replaces the Friday spreadsheet with a live dashboard.

Typical estimate in the guide: 2–6 hours a week. It fits businesses still running on paper, email and spreadsheets. Business automation and websites and dashboards are quoted at a fixed price after the audit. They are not the R8,999 AI-employee starting price.

Planning ranges, not guarantees. Hour ranges in this article are typical estimates for a small South African service business, from the 2026 guide. They are for planning. The audit uses your answers.

Add it up carefully

Most businesses will not run all ten jobs. Even two or three, chosen from the list in the free guide, can free a meaningful part of a week. Start with reply and booking if customers are waiting. Start with follow-up if the enquiries are already in the inbox and going quiet. The order is written up in where to start.
