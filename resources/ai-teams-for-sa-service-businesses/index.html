AI teams for dental practices, estate agencies, brokers and colleges

The same jobs, grouped the way these businesses already work.

AI AutoTech (Pty) Ltd · About 3 min read · From the 2026 guide

Why a team, not a single chatbot

A South African service business rarely has one repetitive job. The practice that misses WhatsApp messages also misses reminders and no-shows. The agency that is slow to answer also loses the viewing follow-up. Grouping the jobs into a team keeps the handoffs in one place.

These four teams are the ones published on this site. They use the jobs in the 2026 guide. They are a starting map, and the audit confirms which roles you actually need.

Dental Front Desk Team

WhatsApp receptionist, booking, reminders, no-show follow-up and reviews. Clinical questions stay with the practice. The team page is Dental Front Desk.

Estate Agent Lead Team

Enquiry responder, buyer and renter qualifier, viewing scheduler and follow-up. Negotiations and mandates stay with the agent. The team page is Estate Agent Leads.

Broker Client Team

Quote requests, document collector, renewal reminders and policy questions answered from your approved wording. The agent does not give advice. The team page is Broker Client.

College Admissions Team

Admissions assistant, application collector, applicant follow-up and campus visits. The team page is College Admissions.

What it costs

AI employees start from R8,999 a month and AI voice agents from R14,999 a month, excl. VAT. Those are the starting prices on the pricing section of this site. WhatsApp, CRM, automation and websites are quoted at a fixed price after the audit.

If your business is none of the four, the job list still applies. Start with Priority 1-2-3 and the ground rules. AI AutoTech (Pty) Ltd is based in Benoni and works with businesses across Ekurhuleni, Johannesburg and Gauteng. You deal with the people who build the system.
