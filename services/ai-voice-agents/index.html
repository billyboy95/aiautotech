How we implement

  1. 01
    Script & voice

    We write the call flow with you: the greeting, the questions to ask, what it may and may not say, and when to transfer to a person.

  2. 02
    Connect your line

    Call forwarding or a dedicated number, linked to your calendar and CRM, with after-hours and overflow rules.

  3. 03
    Test & go live

    We run test calls with you until it sounds right, then launch and review the call summaries with you every week at first.

How you gain

  • Calls answered after hours, over lunch and when everyone is busy
  • Every caller qualified with the same questions, every time
  • Appointments booked straight into your calendar
  • A written summary and transcript of every call, sent to your team
  • Urgent calls transferred to a person immediately
  • Optional outbound follow-up and reminder calls, within POPIA and consent rules

Who it's for

Built for South African businesses that live on enquiries, bookings and follow-ups.

  • Schools & colleges

  • Estate agents

    Answer every call from boards and listings, qualify the caller and book the viewing.

  • Brokers & advisers
