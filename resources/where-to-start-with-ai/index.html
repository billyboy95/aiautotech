Where a South African SME should start with AI

Do the jobs where customers are waiting and money is leaking. Build from there.

AI AutoTech (Pty) Ltd · About 3 min read · From the 2026 guide

Do not automate the whole business in one go

The 2026 guide lists ten jobs. A dental practice, an estate agency, a brokerage or a college does not need all ten on day one. Start where customers are waiting and where work you have already won is going quiet.

The three ground rules still apply on day one: approved information, a person for sensitive work, and POPIA.

Priority 1. Reply and book

Instant WhatsApp replies, booking, and reminders. These are jobs 1, 3 and 4 in the guide. They stop enquiries leaking to the next business on the customer's phone, and you can see whether the diary is filling.

Read first replies on WhatsApp and missed calls, booking and reminders for what each job includes. Voice answering can sit here too if the phone, not WhatsApp, is where you are losing people.

Priority 2. Follow up

No-shows, lead follow-up and documents. Jobs 5, 6 and 7. This is the work that turns enquiries you already have into booked time, viewings, applications or files that are complete enough for a person to act.

No-shows, leads and documents covers the handoff: the agent collects and chases, and licensed or clinical staff still advise.

Priority 3. Grow and run

Voice (if it was not first), renewals, reviews, and the admin around quotes, invoices, CRM updates and reports. Jobs 2, 8, 9 and 10. These compound. A recall that goes out every month is worth more in month six than in week one, and it does not require a new hire to keep doing it.

That set is written up in recalls, reviews and the Friday spreadsheet.

How to choose your own order

The free AI audit asks about your business and returns three things: your top opportunities across sales, marketing, service and operations; a recommended AI team; and a Priority 1-2-3 with time-saving estimates based on your answers. There is no card. The answers are used to prepare the plan.
