First replies on WhatsApp, including after hours and public holidays

The questions are the same every day. The leak is the hour when nobody is at the phone.

AI AutoTech (Pty) Ltd · About 3 min read · From the 2026 guide

The job

Answering “Are you open? How much? Where are you? Do you have…?” straight away, including after hours, weekends and public holidays. For a dental practice in Benoni, an estate agency in Johannesburg or a brokerage anywhere in Gauteng, that first reply is often the whole enquiry.

If the reply waits until morning, the person has already messaged the next practice, the next agent or the next broker. WhatsApp is where that decision happens.

What a small team typically spends on it

In the 2026 guide this job is listed with a typical estimate of 3–8 hours a week for a small South African service business, roughly 2–15 staff. Your number depends on how many of those messages you already get. The free AI audit works it out from your answers. The range is for planning. It is not a promise.

How AI AutoTech sets it up

An AI WhatsApp receptionist, trained on your approved hours, prices, services and wording, connected to one shared team inbox. It answers the common questions, captures the person's name and what they need, and passes anything it should not handle to a person.

The system runs in the cloud, so it keeps replying during load shedding even when the office is dark. That matters in Ekurhuleni and the rest of Gauteng, where the customer does not pause their WhatsApp because your lights are off.

It suits every WhatsApp-first business, and especially dental practices, estate agencies and brokers. The service page for WhatsApp automation covers the shared inbox, lead capture and reminders in more detail.

What stays with your team

Prices and wording you have not approved. Clinical questions. Financial advice. Complaints. Anything the agent is unsure of. The person picks up a chat that already has the basics, instead of a missed message from last night.

Read the three ground rules before you turn this on, then look at missed calls, booking and reminders, which are the jobs that usually sit next to the first reply.
